<b>Requirements (Instruments, Chemicals & Other) : </b><br>

1.  Desiccator <br>
2.  Analytical Balance <br>
3.  Sulphuric acid <br>
4.  Measuring cylinder <br>
5.  Distilled water <br><br>

<b>Procedure :</b><br>

<b>Determination food stability : </b><br>

1.  Prepare 0%, 10%, 30%, 50% and 70% solutions of sulphuric acid and pour it in five different desiccators. <br>
2.	Weigh 10 g bread sample and place in each desiccator. <br>
3.	Weigh and observe the sample after 3 days. <br>
4.	Weigh and observe the sample after 6 days. <br>
5.	Weigh and observe the sample after 9 days. <br>
6.	Record the final weight change and observe the microbial growth in the samples.  <br>
